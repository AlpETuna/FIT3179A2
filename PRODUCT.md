# A place to call home

## Register
brand

## Users
Everyday Australians reading on a laptop or phone, with no assumed statistical training.

## Product Purpose
An original FIT3179 narrative visualisation about the assigned topic, Housing and Renting in Australia. Explain tenure, dwelling forms, regional differences, income, costs and rental assistance using reliable public data.

## Brand Personality
Clear, grounded, humane. Public-interest storytelling with an inviting opening and evidence-led conclusions.

## Anti-references
Not a property-listings site, investment recommendation engine or analyst dashboard. Avoid decorative metric cards, endless filters, fabricated statistics and copied chart-gallery compositions.

## Design Principles
- Every chart answers a distinct reader question.
- Tell the story before offering interaction.
- Distinguish geographical counts, shares, medians and household measures.
- Dates, populations and unknowns belong beside the evidence.
- Include original dwelling illustrations, an opening hook and a synthesis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operated case selection, visible focus, reduced-motion support, chart descriptions and accessible data tables. Essential meaning never depends on colour or hover alone.

## Delivery constraints
All assessed chart rendering uses Vega/Vega-Lite. One scrolling page; readable JSON and local data; GitHub Pages compatible. Local commits are authorised. Push and deployment require the user's explicit approval after review. The hand-drawn sketch and tutor decisions cannot be produced or claimed by the implementation.
